# Siput — character pack

A garden snail. The name means "snail" — slow, steady, and always arriving.
Patience, steady progress, and the work that finishes by never stopping.

Credit: **Siput by I Made Arga Swarsa**. Original design for the lukis
catalog.

Style: **riso**
Cutout chroma: **magenta**
Aliases: snail, keong

## Locked design

- **Body**: a plump snail — a soft rounded body with a large spiral shell
  on its back and two short eyestalks on the head.
- **Face**: two simple dot eyes, one at the tip of each eyestalk, blank
  deadpan (no mouth, no brows, no smile).
- **Shell spiral**: the **accent carrier** — the spiral center of the
  shell, the only accent-colored part.
- A flat crawl foot for a base (no legs — it glides).

## Interaction model

- Contact surfaces: the head end (nudge, press) and the shell side (carry,
  brace, rest loads on it).
- Reach: minimal — contacts happen right beside the body.
- Grip: pressure/contact only; Siput nudges, carries on its shell, or lets
  things rest against it.
- Support/locomotion: the flat crawl foot; Siput glides slowly and may
  climb vertical surfaces — climbing is standing, not dexterity.
- Protected regions: the face interior — only the two dot eyes appear
  there. The eyestalks never hold objects.

## Value rules

- **Riso**: flat ink body with halftone grain; eyes are paper dots; the
  shell spiral carries the accent.
- **Light palettes**: body in pale paper tint with structure-ink outline;
  eyes are structure-ink dots; spiral stays accent.
- **Dark/bold palettes**: body filled with the structure ink; eyes are
  paper dots; spiral stays accent.
