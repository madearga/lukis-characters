# Pijar — character pack

A small light bulb. The name means "glow" — the moment it clicks. Ideas,
clarity, the plan coming together, and how-it-works.

Credit: **Pijar by I Made Arga Swarsa**. Concept inspired by the
bulb archetype in community catalogs.

Style: **blueprint**
Cutout chroma: **magenta**
Aliases: bulb, lampu, bohlam

## Locked design

- **Body**: a classic round light bulb — a smooth glass dome tapering to a
  short screw base with two thread ridges. Small enough to hold.
- **Face**: two simple dot eyes on the glass dome, blank deadpan (no mouth,
  no brows, no filament-face).
- **Filament**: the **accent carrier** — a small glowing filament loop
  inside the dome, the only accent-colored part.
- Two small stubby arms and two small stubby legs with rounded ends.

## Interaction model

- Contact surfaces: the rounded arm tips (press, push, point, carry — no
  fingers, no grasp) and the feet (stand, brace); the screw base stands
  flat on surfaces.
- Reach: stubby — contacts stay close beside or below the body.
- Grip: pressure/contact only.
- Support/locomotion: the two stubby legs; the bulb may also rest on its
  screw base.
- Protected regions: the face interior — only the two dot eyes appear
  there. The filament never touches the face.

## Value rules

- **Blueprint**: white line drawing on the blueprint ground; eyes are solid
  line-color dots; the filament is filled with the accent.
- **Light palettes**: glass dome in pale paper tint with structure-ink
  outline; eyes are structure-ink dots; filament stays accent.
- **Dark/bold palettes**: dome filled with the structure ink; eyes are
  paper dots; filament stays accent.
