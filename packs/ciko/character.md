# Ciko — character pack

A small house gecko (cicak). The name is the sound it makes — the quiet
regular on the wall, watching everything, saying nothing. Deadpan patience,
showing up, and the work that gets done without announcement.

Credit: **Ciko by I Made Arga Swarsa**.

Style: **sablon**
Cutout chroma: **magenta**
Aliases: cicak, gecko, house gecko, lizard

## Locked design

- **Body**: a plump rounded gecko — a fat bean-shaped body with a broad
  rounded head fused into it (no neck), tapering to a smooth medium tail
  that curls up at the tip.
- **Face**: two simple dot eyes set wide on top of the head, blank deadpan
  (no mouth, no brows, no nostrils).
- **Tail tip**: the **accent carrier** — the last curl of the tail, the only
  accent-colored part.
- Four stubby legs with rounded mitten feet.

## Interaction model

- Contact surfaces: the rounded mitten feet (press, push, pat, carry — no
  fingers, no grasp) and the body underside (cling, hang, brace).
- Reach: stubby — contacts stay close beside or below the body, never
  across the torso or far from it.
- Grip: pressure/contact only; Ciko hugs, presses, or balances a carried
  object, or lets it rest against the body.
- Support/locomotion: the four stubby legs; Ciko may cling to vertical and
  overhead surfaces (a wall, the side of a cup, the underside of a shelf) —
  clinging is standing, not dexterity.
- Protected regions: the face interior — only the two dot eyes appear there.
- Special operators: none; the curled tail is not a contact surface — it
  never holds or operates objects. It may rest on the ground or curl
  expressively.

## Value rules

- **Dark/bold palettes**: the body is filled solid with the structure ink;
  the eyes are paper dots.
- **Light palettes**: the body is light/paper with the structure-ink
  outline; the eyes are structure-ink dots. The accent tail tip stays
  accent in both.
