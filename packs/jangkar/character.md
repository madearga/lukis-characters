# Jangkar — character pack

A ship's anchor. The name means "anchor" — holding fast when the sea gets
rough. Stability, commitment, and the weight that keeps things from
drifting.

Credit: **Jangkar by I Made Arga Swarsa**. Original design for the lukis
catalog.

Style: **woodcut**
Cutout chroma: **magenta**
Aliases: anchor

## Locked design

- **Body**: a classic admiralty anchor — a straight shank with a round ring
  at the top, a horizontal stock near the top, and two curved arms ending
  in pointed flukes at the bottom.
- **Face**: two simple dot eyes on the upper shank, blank deadpan (no
  mouth, no brows, no rust spots as features).
- **Ring**: the **accent carrier** — the round anchor ring at the top,
  the only accent-colored part.
- Two small stubby arms with rounded ends (no legs — it stands on its
  flukes).

## Interaction model

- Contact surfaces: the rounded arm tips (hook, hold, drag, brace — no
  fingers, no grasp) and the flukes (dig in, anchor, stand).
- Reach: stubby — contacts stay close beside the body.
- Grip: pressure/contact only; Jangkar hooks, holds, and braces.
- Support/locomotion: the two flukes; Jangkar stands planted and never
  walks — moves are hooking, holding, and bracing.
- Protected regions: the face interior — only the two dot eyes appear
  there. The ring never holds objects (it is the accent, not a handle).

## Value rules

- **Woodcut**: body carved solid ink; eyes left as uncarved paper; the ring
  prints in the accent ink, slightly off-register.
- **Light palettes**: body in pale paper tint with structure-ink outline;
  eyes are structure-ink dots; ring stays accent.
- **Dark/bold palettes**: body filled with the structure ink; eyes are
  paper dots; ring stays accent.
