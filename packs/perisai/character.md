# Perisai — character pack

A heater shield. The name means "shield" — nothing gets past it.
Protection, standing guard, holding the line — security and guarding
prod.

Credit: **Perisai by I Made Arga Swarsa**. Original design for the lukis
catalog.

Style: **enamel**
Cutout chroma: **magenta**
Aliases: shield

## Locked design

- **Body**: a classic heater-shield shape — a broad flat top tapering to a
  rounded point at the bottom.
- **Face**: two simple dot eyes on the upper field, blank deadpan (no
  mouth, no brows, no scratches as features).
- **Boss**: the **accent carrier** — a small round dome boss at the
  center, the only accent-colored part.
- Two small stubby arms with rounded ends (no legs — it stands on its
  point).

## Interaction model

- Contact surfaces: the rounded arm tips (block, hold, present — no
  fingers, no grasp) and the shield face (block, brace).
- Reach: stubby — contacts stay close beside the body.
- Grip: pressure/contact only; Perisai blocks, holds, and presents.
- Support/locomotion: the bottom point; Perisai stands planted and never
  walks — moves are blocking, holding, and presenting.
- Protected regions: the face interior — only the two dot eyes appear
  there. The boss never holds objects (it is the accent, not a handle).

## Value rules

- **Enamel**: deep enamel body with pale enamel eyes, every cell bounded
  by the metal line; the boss is the accent enamel cell.
- **Light palettes**: pale enamel body with deep enamel eyes; boss stays
  accent.
- **Dark/bold palettes**: deep enamel body with pale enamel eyes; boss
  stays accent.
