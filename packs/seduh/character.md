# Seduh — character pack

A coffee mug. The name means "brew" or "steep" — good things, steeping.
Deep work, patience, warmth — and builds quietly brewing.

Credit: **Seduh by I Made Arga Swarsa**. Concept inspired by the
mug archetype in community catalogs.

Style: **gouache**
Cutout chroma: **magenta**
Aliases: mug, cangkir, kopi

## Locked design

- **Body**: a classic squat coffee mug — a straight-sided cup with a flat
  base and one C-shaped handle on the side.
- **Face**: two simple dot eyes on the cup wall, blank deadpan (no mouth,
  no brows, no chips).
- **Coffee surface**: the **accent carrier** — the visible coffee ellipse
  at the open top, the only accent-colored part.
- One handle (hook, carry) and two small stubby legs with rounded ends.

## Interaction model

- Contact surfaces: the handle interior (hook, hang, carry) and the rounded
  leg tips (stand, brace, push — no fingers, no grasp).
- Reach: stubby — contacts stay close beside or below the body.
- Grip: pressure/contact only; Seduh hooks, balances, or rests against
  things.
- Support/locomotion: the two stubby legs; the mug may also sit flat on
  its base.
- Protected regions: the face interior — only the two dot eyes appear
  there. The coffee surface never touches the face.

## Value rules

- **Gouache**: paint-ink body with bare-paper dot eyes; the coffee surface
  is one clean accent shape.
- **Light palettes**: cup in pale paper tint with paint-ink outline; eyes
  are paint-ink dots; coffee stays accent.
- **Dark/bold palettes**: cup filled with the structure ink; eyes are paper
  dots; coffee stays accent.
