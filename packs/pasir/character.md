# Pasir — character pack

An hourglass. The name means "sand" — watching the time slip by, one grain
at a time. Deadlines, timeboxes, patience — and where the time went.

Credit: **Pasir by I Made Arga Swarsa**. Concept inspired by the
hourglass archetype in community catalogs.

Style: **chalk**
Cutout chroma: **magenta**
Aliases: hourglass, jam-pasir

## Locked design

- **Body**: a classic hourglass — two glass bulbs joined at a narrow waist,
  a flat top cap and a flat circular base it stands on.
- **Face**: two simple dot eyes on the upper bulb, blank deadpan (no mouth,
  no brows, no cracks).
- **Sand stream**: the **accent carrier** — the thin falling sand stream
  between the bulbs plus the small sand pile it lands on, the only
  accent-colored parts (one connected accent shape).
- Two small stubby arms with rounded ends (no legs — it stands on its flat
  base).

## Interaction model

- Contact surfaces: the rounded arm tips (press, point, hold, tip — no
  fingers, no grasp) and the flat base (stand, brace).
- Reach: stubby — contacts stay close beside the body.
- Grip: pressure/contact only.
- Support/locomotion: the flat circular base; Pasir stands, never walks —
  moves are pointing, holding, and tipping.
- Protected regions: the face interior — only the two dot eyes appear
  there. The sand stream never touches the face.

## Value rules

- **Chalk**: chalk drawing on the slate board; eyes are solid chalk dots;
  the sand stream is shaded in with the side of the accent chalk.
- **Light palettes**: glass bulbs in pale paper tint with structure-ink
  outline; eyes are structure-ink dots; sand stays accent.
- **Dark/bold palettes**: bulbs filled with the structure ink; eyes are
  paper dots; sand stays accent.
